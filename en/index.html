Document AI · SaaS

TenderIQ

AI analysis of public tender documents, traceable down to the clause

Reads Turkish tender specifications and extracts requirements, required documents, risky clauses, deadlines and the bidder’s eligibility gaps. Every finding is highlighted on the original PDF with document, page and clause. A finding that cannot be tied to a source is never shown.

OSINT · Image analysis

GhostBuilding

Detecting censorship and change from differences between satellite sources

Compares Esri, MapTiler and IGN satellite tiles of the same place, flags differences that are unusual against a baseline measured on ordinary locations (blurring, mosaicking, mismatched imagery), and shows every finding with its evidence tiles.

How I work

The AI agent brings speed. Trust comes from process: decisions on record, rules bound to tests, and measurement on real data.

  1. 1

    Write down the problem and what is out of scope

    Every project starts with an idea document: whose problem it is, what v1 must include, what is deliberately left out.

    ExampleOsintLab: GEOINT and SOCMINT were not opened because they failed the gate.

  2. 2

    Measure the risky decision first

    If a technology or library is unknown to me, I test it with a small spike before writing code; the decision is made with a number.

    ExampleOsintLab: same schema, KùzuDB 353 ms vs Neo4j 3,033 ms. Kokpit: five spawn variants for ConPTY.

  3. 3

    Put decisions on record

    A final document and architecture decision records (ADRs). The AI agent does not rebuild context every session; it reads the same record.

    ExampleFabrikaOS: 24 ADRs, a design document and implementation plan per sprint.

  4. 4

    Turn safety rules into tests

    A phased roadmap, each phase with a measurable “done” criterion. Rules of the “if this is wrong, data leaks” kind are CI gates, not sentences.

    ExampleAEGIS: if the tenant-leak test or the 100% recall gate is red, nothing merges.

  5. 5

    Run it end to end on real data

    “Tests pass” and “the product works” are separate measurements. Before calling it done, I run the product start to finish on a real input.

    ExampleTenderIQ: its model had been retired; a run on a real tender caught it, not the unit tests.
