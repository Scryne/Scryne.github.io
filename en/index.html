    Detecting censorship and change from differences between satellite sources

How I work

  1. 01

    Write down the problem and what is out of scope

    OsintLab: GEOINT and SOCMINT were not opened because they failed the gate.

  2. 02

    Measure the risky decision first

    OsintLab: same schema, KùzuDB 353 ms vs Neo4j 3,033 ms. Kokpit: five spawn variants for ConPTY.

    Put decisions on record

    FabrikaOS: 24 ADRs, a design document and implementation plan per sprint.
