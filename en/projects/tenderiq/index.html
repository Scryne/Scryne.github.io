TenderIQ

AI analysis of public tender documents, traceable down to the clause

Reads Turkish tender specifications and extracts requirements, required documents, risky clauses, deadlines and the bidder’s eligibility gaps. Every finding is highlighted on the original PDF with document, page and clause. A finding that cannot be tied to a source is never shown.

The problem

A bid team searches a 300-page tender for one thing: which clause disqualifies us or puts us at risk, and exactly where is it written? A chat model answers fluently but cannot show the clause it relied on, which is not enough for a decision with legal consequences.

What it does

  • Grounded findings: five specialist agents (requirements, documents, risk, timeline, eligibility) run in one LangGraph flow. A finding that cannot be tied to a source passage never reaches the report; the rule lives in code, not in the prompt.
  • Any format: digital and scanned PDF (OCR), DOCX, XLSX. Retrieval is BGE-M3 + BM25 with RRF fusion and reranking.
  • Human in the loop: every finding is approved, corrected or rejected; decisions are reversible, audited, and export to Word and Excel with their sources.
  • Eligibility: the company’s capability profile is matched against the tender; on the demo tender it flags the missing ISO 27001.
  • SaaS: tenant isolation with PostgreSQL RLS, roles, plans and quotas; a per-tenant LLM budget cap.

Measurements

Struck through: what was found. Beside it: measured after the fix.

How it was built with AI

  • AI quality is a CI gate: a golden-set evaluation blocks CI whenever a prompt or model changes.
  • Model choice by measurement: when the development model was retired by its provider (410 Gone), five candidates were tried; the successor returned 4/4 findings with verbatim quotes on the test tender.
  • Decisions on record: every architectural choice is an ADR; every session starts from DURUM.md.

September 2026 audit

When re-measured, the tests were green but the product did not work: the model had been retired, CI had been red for a month (2 critical + 6 high advisories), one empty setting disabled six test files, and a raw “Failed to fetch” showed in ten places. After: CI 9/9 green, 0 high/critical in trivy, 18 of 18 routes at 100 for accessibility, 34 cited findings on a fictional tender.

Takeaway

A system that depends on an external model dies quietly when that model is retired. A unit test does not catch it; an end-to-end run on a real document does.

Honest limits

  • No hosted demo; the product is ready for closed beta but has no customers yet.
  • Live billing and legal terms are not wired up yet.
