AEGIS

Know-your-business and due diligence for AML-regulated firms in Turkey

Verifies a client company’s identity, screens the company and its directors against sanctions, PEP and asset-freeze lists on a self-hosted engine, records the analyst’s decision with its rationale, and produces an audit-ready, source-linked case file.

The problem

Firms regulated by Turkey’s financial crimes authority (first target: accounting and audit practices) must screen every new corporate client and its directors against sanctions, PEP and asset-freeze lists, and defend the decision in an audit. Today this is manual; Turkish spelling differences (“A.Ş.” vs “Anonim Şirketi”, İ/ı) let real matches slip, and “why did you dismiss this?” is rarely on record.

What it does

  • Entity register: company, directors, beneficial owners, each value with its source and time. ID numbers in an encrypted column, duplicates found via a blind index.
  • Suggestions from documents: a signature circular is turned into text with Docling and directors arrive as suggestions; nothing enters the register unapproved.
  • Local screening: a self-hosted OpenSanctions engine; personal data never leaves. Results fall into three bands: reasoned auto-dismissal, review, urgent.
  • Reasoned decisions, sealed file: the analyst sees each candidate with match reasons and dismisses with a written rationale; the case file is sealed with SHA-256 and the audit trail is hash-chained.

Design

A light, calm workspace; colour carries risk state only. Each screen shows the result, then the rationale, then the action; values an auditor compares are monospaced.
